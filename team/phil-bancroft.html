Hi, my name is Phil, and I’m an integrative counsellor registered with the NCPS. I offer online counselling sessions for individuals in the UK.

I support people who are going through difficulties such as anxiety, depression, low self-esteem, bereavement, or times when life feels overwhelming. My approach is integrative, meaning I combine different therapeutic approaches to best meet each person’s needs. My way of working is guided by you, and by what feels relevant and meaningful to you.

During my training to become a counsellor, I experienced a significant personal loss. Going through grief while learning how to support others gave me a deeper understanding of the realities and complexities of emotional pain and healing. This experience deepened my appreciation of how challenging our emotions can be. It reinforced my commitment to creating a safe space where people can speak openly about what they are going through and feel genuinely heard and understood.

I offer a calm and supportive space where you can bring whatever is on your mind. Together, we can explore your thoughts, feelings, and experiences, helping you better understand yourself, make sense of what you’re going through, and develop practical ways to support your well-being.
