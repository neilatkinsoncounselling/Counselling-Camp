Hello,I'm Neil Atkinson,an integrative counsellor and NCPS registered member offering online counselling to clients across the UK with individuals facing a wide range of challenges--from anxiety and low mood to relationship difficulties,trauma,and life transitions. I draw on person-centred therapy,CBT,and attachment theory,always adapting my approach to what works best for you rather than following a one-size-fits-all method. My path to counselling was a deeply personal one. I lost a close colleague and friend to suicide,and that loss changed me. It made me want to do something meaningful--to help normalise conversations about mental health and make professional support more accessible to the people who need it most. That commitment sits at the heart of everything I do.
