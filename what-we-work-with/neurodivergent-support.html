Neurodivergent Support

Your brain works differently. That is not a problem to be fixed, but it can mean the world does not always fit.

Neurodivergent people, including those with ADHD, autism, dyslexia and related differences, often find that standard counselling approaches do not quite work for them. We adapt how we work to fit the person rather than expecting the person to fit a fixed model. Whether you have a formal diagnosis or simply know that your experience of the world is different, support is available. Our guides below explore what that support can look like.
