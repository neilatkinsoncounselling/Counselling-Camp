Areas of support

What we help with

Whatever has brought you here, you don't need to have the words for it yet. Browse the areas below or book a free consultation and we will help you find the right fit.

  • Anxiety and Stress

    "My mind won't stop."

    You don't have to live in survival mode. We'll help you understand what's driving your anxiety.

    Find out more

  • Depression

    "I don't know how to explain it. I just feel flat."

    Depression can make everything feel heavy and distant. Counselling offers a space to understand what's happening and find a way through.

    Find out more

  • Grief and Loss

    "Everyone expects me to be over it."

    Grief has no timetable. We offer a compassionate, unhurried space to sit with your loss.

    Find out more

  • Trauma

    "Something happened and I've never been the same."

    Trauma can shape how you think, feel and relate to others. Trauma-informed counselling offers a safe path to understanding and healing.

    Find out more

  • Men's Mental Health

    "I should be able to handle this."

    You don't have to. We offer a space where men can speak honestly, without judgement.

    Find out more

  • Neurodivergent Support

    "Finally, someone gets it."

    Your brain works differently. We offer a space where that is understood, not pathologised.

    Find out more
